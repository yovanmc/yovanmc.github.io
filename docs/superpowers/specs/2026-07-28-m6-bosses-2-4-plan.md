## Owner rulings (locked 2026-07-28, this session)

- **Progression model: menu-return.** Victory lands in the menu world (M5's
  shipped beat + teaser stays); the next undefeated boss sits behind `FIGHT`.
  No boss chains directly into the next. "Boss rush" = fixed order + per-victory
  unlocks, not an unbroken gauntlet.
- **Rider carry-over is DERIVED, not stored:** `initBattle` computes
  `maxHp = 100 + 10·D`, `maxMp = 10 + 2·D` where D = `defeatedBosses.length`.
  Retry keeps the rider automatically; rematches of earlier bosses now start at
  the hero's current (post-rider) stats — the addendum's "permanently raises
  stats", now actually permanent. (Shipped M5 rematch started 100/10; that
  changes, and the victory-time rider event stays as the level-up presentation.)
- **Cross-visit persistence stays deferred to M4** (localStorage progression
  lands with the unlock UI). Until then progression is per page load: every
  fresh visit's dive lands in the Alert Storm fight, and the rush is climbed
  within the visit. Recorded as deferred, not forgotten.
- **One plan doc for all three bosses** (this one), three per-boss numbers
  tables inside it. The Cascade table is signed before PR-1; the Silent Failure
  and Imposter tables are re-confirmed at their PR starts so earlier playtests
  can re-tune them before code exists.

### Multipliers (extends M5's pinned micro-semantics; dissect F2 — ONE rule)

- **Dealt:** `round(base × (conv ? 2 : 1) × (ct ? (conv ? 2.0 : 1.5) : 1))`,
  round half up applied once, after everything. Conviction REPLACES CT's
  percentage (+50% becomes +100%); it never stacks a second CT factor.
  Worked example, pinned as a named test: PT under both = 28 × 2 × 2 = 112.
- **Taken:** `round(base × (ct ? (conv ? 0.5 : 0.75) : 1))` — again Conviction
  replaces the CT percentage (−25% becomes −50%); with CT down, Conviction
  alone reduces nothing. Worked example, pinned: glitch slash under both =
  14 × 0.5 = 7.
- Conviction doubles the CONSTANTS of every other ability (PT 56, FO 16,
  Debug hit 12, RC 44, Rollback heal 60) and Debug's DoT tick 4→8 for DoTs
  applied after activation; already-running DoTs keep their tick value. DoTs
  are still never CT-multiplied.
- Healing (Rollback) caps at max HP; MP regen caps at max MP (unchanged).

### Renderer strategy per boss

- **Cascade — region composition (new problem, solved without new art):** the
  lab has no per-node primitive, and the engine kills ARBITRARY node sets
  while `cascadeDark(k)` only draws first-k-dead. Pass-1 verified the box
  geometry is sound (node boxes are disjoint and non-adjacent, `eOutline` is
  local, and `cascadeDark(6, bob)` aligns cell-for-cell with
  `cascadeFinal(f)` at `bob = (f+i)%2`) and found three holes (F4), all
  specified here rather than left to the builder:
  - **Per-node box composition with a bob-parity source selector.** No single
    `cascadeFinal(f)` frame has a fixed lit node with flipped bobs (lit and
    bob share `f`), so a naive base-frame approach renders a STILL chain. The
    composer instead builds each frame per node: for flutter phase `p`, node
    `i` needs bob parity `(p+i)%2`; a selector picks a source frame `f` with
    matching parity and `f ∉ {i, (i+1) mod 6}` (pass-2 G3: excluding only
    `f = i` still admits frames where node i is the AFTERGLOW node —
    `cascadeFinal` gives the node the pulse just left a `W` core — so both
    the lit and the after slot are excluded; ≥1 valid frame survives per
    parity). Living unlit nodes copy from there; the pulse-carrier's box +
    core comes from `cascadeFinal(q)` where **q = the engine's carrier node
    index** (the carrier doesn't bob; it's holding the charge); dead nodes
    copy from the memoized `cascadeDark(6, bob)` husks.
  - **Links are drawn from engine state, not inherited.** `cascadeFinal`
    draws all five links unconditionally and its hot-link rule (`i === f−1`)
    cannot express a pulse hopping over husks; 11 of the 15 link dots land
    inside node boxes and would be half-erased by husk copies. The composer
    draws link dots itself between LIVING-neighbor node centers (same 4-step
    interpolation the lab uses), hot edge = the engine's actual last hop —
    the engine exposes `lastHop` for exactly this. Renderer-drawn connective
    tissue is the M5 mark-chevron precedent, not retyped art.
  - **The composer is a pure, unit-tested function** — not screenshot-gated
    only. `composeCascade` lives in `src/battle/bosses/cascade.ts` (a `.ts`
    file so the widened coverage gate measures it — pass-2 G10) and vitest
    asserts per-box deep-equality against its source frames, links only
    between living neighbors, zero stray cells outside boxes + links, AND
    the semantic assertion pass-2 G3 demanded (per-box equality alone is
    blind to wrong-source selection): every living unlit node's core cell
    is `N` — never a stray `X` lit core or `W` afterglow.
  Storm telegraph/fire = `CAS_ATK` overload reel; boss hit = `CAS_HIT`;
  death = `CAS_DIE` when the last node falls. Arena = `varCC`.
- **Silent Failure — monolithic reels are CORRECT here** (single entity, no
  per-entity state): engine phase drives frame family (body/empty/fade
  transitions from `SIL_REEL` parts), `SIL_ATK` on the ambush, `SIL_HIT`
  (body only), `SIL_DIE`. While hidden with the mark: purple mote overlay at
  the armor position (renderer overlay, like the M5 mark chevron — betrays
  position visually, grants nothing mechanically). Arena = `varSF` (footing
  safe zones are already audit-enforced in the lab).
- **Imposter — hero-grid stamps + facing + erosion:** `IMP_REEL` idle,
  `IMP_ATK`, `IMP_HIT` (band-tear), `IMP_DIE` (shred to static).
  **Facing (dissect F5 — the battlefield spec's open per-boss item, and
  unavoidable for the one boss built from the hero's own frames):** the boss
  stands at `BOSS_AT` (left side) and the Imposter IS the hero's art, so
  unmirrored it faces away from the hero; no mirror helper exists anywhere in
  the pipeline. Decision row in the Imposter table: renderer-owned
  `mirrorOf(grid)` (per-row reverse) applied AFTER `remapOf` and with every
  overlay coordinate mirrored too (`eyes()` hardcodes cols 23/27 — a naive
  reverse puts the red eyes on the wrong side), unit-tested; the alternative
  (owner may prefer it) is ruling that the dark mirror deliberately shares
  the hero's facing. Clone phase renders three imposter silhouettes (real =
  full render; clones = `hDither`/glitch variants) at spread offsets; the
  60×48 sprites at ±20-col offsets overflow the 60×64 boss zone deliberately
  (visual spread across the disc) — layout is a wireframe decision for the
  first screenshot gate. Arena: stage 0 uses `varIS(ph)` (the live variant —
  `erosionStage(0, ·)` drops its scanline and freezes its glitch-band flutter,
  dissect F12), stages 1–3 use `erosionStage(t, ph)`, all 4×2 frames
  memoized; the victory beat plays `IMP_DIE` over the PURE station (stage 3),
  which is the finale the battlefield spec locked.

Supporting arithmetic, **corrected after dissect pass 1 H1** — the figures
first shown at signing were off because they charged the hero a boss turn on
the killing turn, when victory resolves before the boss acts. No signed row
changes value; only the illustrative endpoints move. Both lines re-derived
from the shipped constants (`ATTACK_DMG 12`, `PT_DMG 28`, `DEBUG_DMG 6`,

- **Intended line, 7 hero turns, ends 46/120.** PT, Debug (its DoT ticks
  through the vanish for free), whiff the first vanish turn, CT on the LAST
  vanish turn so the buff covers both embodied turns AND triggers the
  window extension, then PT ×2 CT'd and PT once more in the extended third
  turn. Damage taken 12+12+18+14+9+9 = 74.
- **Naive PT-only line, 9 hero turns, ends 30/120 with ONE Rollback.** Five
  PTs across three embodied windows, no CT, one Rollback when the second
  vanish window bites. Damage taken 12+12+18+18+12+12+18+18 = 120 against
  120 max HP plus the 30 heal.
- The two lines differ on what the vanish turns are FOR: the intended line
  spends them on the whiff and the CT setup, the naive line spends them on
  Rollback sustain. Both are legal; the table's Rollback row describes the
  latter.
- Degenerate play loses. Attack-spam: 140/12 = 12 embodied turns = 6 full
  cycles = 360 incoming. Rollback-stalling: MP regen 1/turn against a 3 MP
  heal is 10 HP/turn of sustain against ~15 HP/turn of incoming.

- **Fastest legal line, 6 hero turns, ends 55/120** (found by dissect pass 2
  J9, below the band floor first signed). PT, PT, whiff, CT, PT, PT: 28 + 28
  + 42 + 42 = exactly 140. MP is feasible (14 start, 3+3+0+2+3+3 = 14 spent
  against 14 + 5 regen) and SF is deterministic, so this line always works.
  Damage taken 12+12+18+14+9 = 65. It skips Debug and Rollback entirely.

Measured lines therefore land at **6 to 9** turns; the signed band is 6 to 11,
keeping headroom for slower play. No mechanic is unreachable and nothing
trivialises the fight — the 6-turn line still enters both phases and still
eats a full vanish window, so this is not the Cascade v1 failure (dissect F1),
where every mechanic was unreachable. What it does mean is that Debug and
Rollback are OPTIONAL on this boss rather than load-bearing; recorded as a
playtest question, not a re-open of the signed numbers.

## PR-2 architecture decisions (settled at PR-2 start, 2026-07-28)

Four decisions the v3 plan left to implementation time, settled here from a
measured read of PR-1a/PR-1b's shipped seams so the builder never guesses.

### D1 — the two-arm dispatch does not survive a third boss (measured)

Every per-boss seam shipped in PR-1a/1b is a TWO-arm shape,
`if (boss.kind === CASCADE_ID) { cascade } else { alert storm }`, where the
`else` IS the Alert Storm path — not a default, not exhaustive, no `never`
guard. Measured sites: `engine.ts` `initBattle`'s boss construction,
`cloneBoss`, `findTarget`, `dealSingleTarget`, `markTarget`, the `fo` case,
the boss-turn block, the DoT-tick loop (two sites), the `s.dots` filter, and
the victory block's `bossDefeated` + `forgeAbility` ternaries; `BattleScene.tsx`
`revealBoss`, `livingCount`, `plateHp`, `batCell`, `cursorCell`,
`livingByColumn`. Adding a third `kind` to that shape is silently wrong at
every site not edited: the Silent Failure falls into the Alert Storm arm and
`tsc` reports nothing, on an auto-deploy repo.

**The `BattleScene` half is WORSE than the `engine` half (dissect pass 1 H2,
measured by live probe, not inferred).** Stubbing a third `BossState` member
and running `npx tsc -b` produces **13 errors, all of them in `engine.ts` and
ZERO in `BattleScene.tsx`**. The reason: none of those six `BattleScene` sites
narrows on `boss.kind` itself — they all go through the helpers `alertBats`
and `cascadeNodes` (`BattleScene.tsx:38-45`), each a two-arm ternary
(`boss.kind === "alert-storm" ? boss.bats : []`) whose `else` branch never
dereferences a kind-specific property. A third boss therefore type-checks
CLEAN and silently receives an empty target collection, which routes straight
into `livingByColumn` returning `[]` and `startTarget`/`cycleTarget`
(`BattleScene.tsx:396-414`) leaving the cursor with no home — precisely the
failure D2 exists to prevent. **`alertBats` and `cascadeNodes` are therefore
named D1 conversion sites in their own right**, and task 1's probe must show
failures at each site individually, never just an aggregate count.

**Two corrections from dissect pass 2:**

- **`cursorBatObj` / `cursorNodeObj` (`BattleScene.tsx:516-527`) is a site
  too, and it is the one that bites hardest (J4).** Both are `isCascadeFight`
  ternaries. For the Silent Failure, `cursorBatObj` is `undefined` and
  `cursorNodeObj` is `null`, so `cursorTargetId` is `null`, the cursor arrow
  (`BattleScene.tsx:608`) never renders and `cursorRead` is `""` for the
  entire fight — an invisible target cursor. It type-checks clean even AFTER
  task 1, because the exhaustiveness errors land inside `alertBats` /
  `cascadeNodes` and filling their SF arms with `[]` re-silences these call
  sites: D1's own failure class, one layer up. Committing still works (Enter
  reads `stateRef.current.cursorBat`), so the interactive gate's scripted line
  and the keyboard fight both PASS while the fight is unplayable by cursor.
  Task 6 fills it, and §Verification gains a target-mode capture beat, because
  no existing beat would have caught this.
- **`initBattle`'s boss construction is EXEMPT from the never-guard (J2).**
  That site dispatches on `requestedBoss: string`, not on `BossState["kind"]`
  — `IMPLEMENTED_BOSSES` is `readonly string[]` with no literal types, so
  `never`-narrowing is impossible there, and the stub probe confirms the site
  contributes zero of the 13 errors. Its trailing `else` is not laziness: it
  is the pinned G1 anti-crash fallback (`engine.ts:109-113`), asserted by
  `engine.test.ts:592-597` (`"not-a-real-boss"` boots Alert Storm). **That
  default is KEPT.** A new boss is added as an `else if` ahead of it, never by
  removing it.

**Decision: PR-2 opens with a pure-refactor commit** converting all of them to
exhaustive dispatch with a `never`-typed exhaustiveness guard, so a fourth boss
(PR-3) fails the TYPE CHECK at every unhandled site instead of mis-routing.
This is PR-1a's proven two-commit pattern reused: refactor with assertions
untouched, then behaviour. It is also the cheapest possible insurance for PR-3,
which adds the boss with the most seams.

### D2 — targetability model: the armor stays selectable, the reducer refuses

The empty armor is on screen during the vanished phase, so the target cursor
keeps exactly one selectable position for the whole fight (`livingByColumn`
returns the single boss target while the boss is alive, vanished or not). The
untargetable rule is enforced in the REDUCER, not by emptying the cursor:

- `pt`, `debug` against a vanished boss → `invalid` (no MP, no turn).
- `fo` while vanished → `invalid` (no MP, no turn) — the signed row.
- `attack` while vanished → resolves as a WHIFF: turn consumed, 0 damage, no
  `+1 MP` (attack's MP gain is "on hit"), a `damage` event with `amount: 0`.

Rejected alternative: emptying the target list while vanished. It reads
cleaner but leaves the player with no way to commit the whiff the signed table
requires, and it breaks the shell's invariant that an alive boss always has a
cursor home.

**Exact implementation sites (pinned after pass-1 H5 — these do NOT fall out
of D1's per-boss branch points, so without pinning they would be a builder
guess).** Today `attack`'s `+1 MP` (`engine.ts:324`) is unconditional and the
pre-validate block checks target existence for `attack`/`pt`/`debug` but never
for `fo`:

- **`pt` / `debug` while vanished** → extend the existing pre-validate gate so
  a target that exists but is not TARGETABLE returns `invalid` with reason
  `"target is not there"`. The targetability question is answered by the boss
  module (`isTargetable(boss)`), never by a `kind` check written inline at the
  gate. **`attack` must be EXEMPTED from that extension (pass-2 J8):** the
  shipped gate (`engine.ts:298-301`) covers `attack|pt|debug` in ONE branch,
  so adding `isTargetable` there without excluding `attack` blocks the whiff
  the signed table requires. Task 4's whiff test would catch it, but the trap
  costs a bisect cycle for one sentence of prevention.
- **`fo` while vanished** → `fo` has no pre-validate gate today (it is
  untargeted). Add its check at the TOP of the `fo` case, before the MP
  deduction, returning `invalid` — not inside the SF arm of the fan-out
  helper, which runs after MP is already spent.
- **`attack` while vanished** → reaches `dealSingleTarget`, whose SF arm
  resolves 0 damage. Suppress the MP grant GENERICALLY by gating it on damage
  actually landing (`amount > 0`) rather than adding a `kind` check at
  `engine.ts:324` — "+1 MP on hit" is what the ability text already promises,
  so this is the rule the code should always have had, and it stays correct
  for PR-3's vanish phase for free.
- Event stream for the whiff: one `damage` event with `amount: 0`, no `mark`,
  no `batDown`, turn consumed. The float renderer must not be asked to
  animate a zero.

### D3 — the plate label must become phase-aware, additively

Corrected after dissect pass 1 H3 — the first draft of this decision
misdescribed the shipped code. What `BattleScene` actually does today:
`scene.plate.label` is rendered **unconditionally** (`BattleScene.tsx:647`),
and `revealBoss` gates only the HP-bar-versus-`hiddenLabel` swap
(`BattleScene.tsx:649-653`). There is no label/HP coupling to decouple.

What the Silent Failure needs is a label that CHANGES WITH PHASE while the HP
bar stays visible throughout (`revealBoss` is simply always true for this
boss). So the change is additive, not a reshape:

- `ScenePlate` keeps `label: string` and `hiddenLabel: string` exactly as
  they are, and grows an OPTIONAL `labelFor?(state: BattleState): string`.
- `BattleScene` renders `plate.labelFor?.(state) ?? plate.label`.
- Alert Storm and Cascade do not implement `labelFor`, so their rendered
  output is byte-identical and `alertStorm.test.ts:99`
  (`expect(alertStormScene.plate.label).toBe("ALERT STORM")`, a property
  access) and `punctuation.test.ts:18` (`value: scene.plate.label`) both keep
  passing unmodified.

**Turning `label` into a method instead would break both of those tests**, and
task 6's "existing tests staying green" would then be unachievable rather than
reassuring — which is exactly the trap pass-1 H3 caught.

**The punctuation gate must be extended in the same commit**, or the one new
string this decision exists to add (`VANISHED`) is the one string never
checked: `punctuation.test.ts`'s `copyStringsFor` iterates
`SCENE_MODULES` generically and reads `plate.label` only, so a `labelFor`-only
string slips the net and silently voids dissect F16's promise to cover plate
labels. Extend `copyStringsFor` so that when `plate.labelFor` exists it boots
`initBattle({ seed: 42, boss: scene.id })` and pushes the result of
`labelFor` for BOTH phase values (embodied and vanished, the vanished one
built by overriding `phase` on the state), each as its own labelled entry.

### D4 — Silent Failure state shape (single entity, target id 0)

```ts
export interface SilentFailureBoss {
  kind: "silent-failure";
  hp: number; maxHp: number;      // 140 / 140
  marked: boolean;                // Debug's mark, permanent for this fight
  phase: "embodied" | "vanished";
  phaseTurnsLeft: number;
  extendedThisWindow: boolean;    // one CT extension per embodied window, max 3
  forceBodyForDeath: boolean;     // set when a DoT tick kills it while vanished
}
```

Target id **0** is the boss itself (the engine's `batId`/`targetId` channel and
the DoT anchor both key off it), matching how the single-entity case degenerates
from Alert Storm's `bats[]` and Cascade's `nodes[]`. `livingTargets` returns
`[0]` while alive, `[]` when dead.

Cycle resolution, pinned so the CT-extension rule is unambiguous. On each boss
turn: deal the phase's damage (embodied 12, vanished 18, both through
`takenDamage` for the CT/Conviction multiplier), THEN decrement
`phaseTurnsLeft`. When it reaches 0:

- embodied **and** `ctTurns > 0` (read here, in the boss phase, BEFORE the
  engine's end-of-turn `ctTurns` decrement) **and** `!extendedThisWindow` →
  `phaseTurnsLeft = 1`, `extendedThisWindow = true` (window becomes 3, capped);
- otherwise → flip `phase`, `phaseTurnsLeft = 2`, `extendedThisWindow = false`.

Vanished windows are always exactly 2 and never extend. Spawn state is
`embodied`, `phaseTurnsLeft: 2`. The two worked cases from the signed table
must both become tests: CT cast on the LAST vanish turn extends the following
window to 3; CT cast on the FIRST vanish turn has expired by the window-end
check and extends nothing.

### D5 — boss frames come from `composeBoss`, NOT from a `reels` field

The v3 plan and the first draft of task 6 both said to attach SF's
`SIL_ATK`/`SIL_HIT`/`SIL_DIE` "the way `scenes/cascade.ts` attaches its
`reels` field". **That instruction was a no-op and is withdrawn (dissect pass
2 J6, measured):** `cascadeScene.reels` (`scenes/cascade.ts:41,55`) has ZERO
consumers — `reels` appears in `BattleScene.tsx` only inside a comment, and
`CAS_ATK`/`CAS_HIT`/`CAS_DIE` appear nowhere outside `scenes/cascade.ts`. The
shell animates the boss exclusively through `composeBoss(boss, screaming,
flutter, fx)`. Mirroring the attachment would therefore have shipped a Silent
Failure whose attack, hit and death frames never play — and the signed
DoT-kill ruling ("re-embodies for the death reel") plus the whole
`forceBodyForDeath` flag would have had no playback path, while the static
victory-overlay screenshot passed vacuously.

**Decision: SF's frame selection lives inside `composeBoss`**, which picks the
family from `(phase, fx, status)` — body vs empty from `phase`, hit/flash from
the `fx` flags the shell already passes, and the `SIL_DIE` family when the
fight is won, forced to the BODY family when `forceBodyForDeath` is set. The
`reels` field is NOT attached to the SF scene module. Rejected alternative:
building a shell-side reel sequencer that consumes `scene.reels`. It is real
unplanned work, and it would light up Cascade's dormant reels as a side
effect — a behaviour change to a shipped boss, outside PR-2's scope.

**Builder instruction:** read `scenes/alertStorm.ts`'s `composeBoss` first and
mirror how it reads `fx` and the victory/defeat states. If the shell turns out
to provide no hook for a victory-state frame at all, **STOP and report** —
do not build a sequencer to fill the gap.
